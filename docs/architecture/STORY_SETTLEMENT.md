# Story Settlement

Story Settlement is Directive's sole semantic chronology. Its job is compression with provenance: preserve the few developments that will matter later without recording every exchange.

One active episode may accumulate multiple accepted contributions and typed effects. Checkpoints are review opportunities, not story entries. A bounded working capsule retains a short evidence window and a concise foreground question. A deterministic hard boundary or validated soft boundary seals the episode. Sealing removes the mutable capsule and retains a summary, lasting effects, unresolved consequences, optional character moments, and exact source references.

A scene is significant only when it contains a lasting change, meaningful disclosure, authored mission effect, unresolved consequence, or another validated significance criterion. Routine acknowledgements create only an insignificant-settlement receipt. They do not appear in the player's story history.

Source mutation invalidates the affected contribution. Derived effects are removed or superseded, dependent episodes are repaired or invalidated, and projections rebuild. No prose is regenerated during deterministic repair. If surviving effects still matter, a source-backed supersession episode may preserve them.

The exact contract requires boundary state, references, character moments, and a working capsule on every current episode. Terminal episodes cannot retain a working capsule. Incomplete records are rejected, not normalized.
