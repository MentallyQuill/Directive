# First Campaign Workflow

1. Enable Directive in SillyTavern.
2. Click the ship icon beside the composer.
3. On Campaign, choose Ashes of Peace. Preview campaigns remain locked.
4. Complete the four character-creation sections and accept the dossier.
5. Let Directive create the Ashes campaign chat and post the opening scene.
6. Respond normally in prose. You do not need command syntax.
7. Swipe assistant replies until you find the version you want. The chosen reply becomes accepted only when you send your next message.
8. Use Mission for clear objectives and known deadlines, People for crew memory and Command Bearing, and Ship for the operational aggregate, system states, gameplay effects, and known narrative ship work.
9. Pursue Ship work naturally in chat when you have the time, access, people, or evidence the fiction requires. There is no project button, and an unlocked capability creates an option rather than an automatic success.
10. Create a checkpoint from Campaign before a major decision if you want an exact V1 snapshot and cloned chat. Loading it restores that state into the active timeline and opens a new playable continuation.

Directive will interpret varied prose against the active mission's authored evidence policies. You never need to click objective checkboxes or manually confirm ordinary story events.
