# Ashes V1: Open Orders III Migration Plan

> Status: approved implementation work under the standing V1 architecture scope. This plan covers non-UI content and deterministic authority; it does not authorize UI or narrator-prompt changes.

**Goal:** Make `open-orders-3-before-the-lamps-go-out` the eleventh V1-native Ashes journey entry while preserving a calm, reflective preparation interval, a normal two-assignment load, Imani's independent agency, and campaign-critical distributed readiness without turning quiet scenes, false-alert tests, every officer's finale assignment, or each asset effect into separate trackers.

**Identity:** Treat Open Orders III as a source-authored V1-only interval. Bind it to package version `0.3.0-pre-alpha.1`, the exact Chapter 7 transition target, and the exact existing package quest successor `chapter-8-the-last-directive`. Do not add a duplicate legacy quest row for the interval. The existing empty legacy side quests `side-the-name-on-the-hull`, `side-a-signal-toward-home`, and `side-two-signatures` remain migration inputs, not parallel schedulers or authorities.

## Player-Safe Opening and Quiet Frame

Initially show:

- the coordinated warning already established after Chapter 7;
- three available assignments: The Name on the Hull, A Signal Toward Home, and Two Signatures;
- that completing two is a normal command load;
- that completing all three requires credible delegation, earlier support, or accepting fatigue and delay;
- a required interval responsibility to prepare and explicitly conclude distributed command readiness before the next operation.

Do not initially reveal:

- that multiple alert systems will activate in the same thirty-second interval;
- Chapter 8's exact false alerts, mutually exclusive orders, node quorum, front assignments, or resolution strategies;
- an automatic moral answer for the remembrance dispute;
- direct Voyager contact or the decisive Pathfinder breakthrough;
- that the Utopia Planitia legal office is malicious;
- that one Imani may consent for the other or that the player can decide Imani's identity;
- a hidden objective, percentage, generic urgency row, or countdown.

At least one ordinary shipboard scene may contain no campaign clue, hidden crisis, or mission evidence. Reflection, a meal, a quiet command conversation, rules-of-engagement drafting, mentorship, triage discussion, or repair prioritization remains Story Settlement material unless it produces one of the bounded aggregate results below.

## Minimal Interval Authority

Use one required objective and three visible conditional-optional objectives:

1. **Conclude distributed readiness** - after the critical readiness report is known, explicitly conclude after a normal two-assignment load, delegated broad coverage, direct overextension, or informed early departure.
2. **The Name on the Hull** - optional public-memory and institutional-trust assignment.
3. **A Signal Toward Home** - optional disciplined long-range relay calibration assignment.
4. **Two Signatures** - optional support, consent, rights, and documented engineering-use assignment.

Selection, delegation, or discussion is not completion. Decline remains reversible until interval conclusion. An assignment result may settle only after its aggregate report is player-known. The required interval conclusion may settle only after the aggregate distributed-readiness report is player-known.

Resolving two assignments is normal. Resolving all three requires at least one credible delegation for broad coverage. Resolving all three under direct command records overextension. Early departure remains failure-forward after the critical readiness report, with unfinished opportunities explicitly carried forward.

## Aggregate Reports

### The Name on the Hull

One required report establishes that the remembrance dispute is genuine rather than secretly manipulated: organizers want a Starfleet symbol, local survivors object to institutional mythology that obscures recent abandonment, and Whitaker is uncomfortable with both refusal and uncritical acceptance.

The actual result may earn **Breckenridge Memorial Goodwill** when the process creates honest shared ownership and credible public trust. Responsible refusal, limited participation, separate remembrance, or another bounded disposition may resolve without the asset. Deliberate exploitation, silencing, or knowingly false institutional mythology after the account is known may fail after informed action. The system must not grade one speech formula or require the player to speak for Whitaker.

### A Signal Toward Home

One required report establishes the completed calibration account: this is a modest signal-coherence experiment connected to broader long-range work, not decisive Pathfinder and not direct Voyager contact; it records actual beacon, formation, phase-distortion, research-station, and contamination findings at an aggregate level.

The actual result may earn **Long-Range Relay Window** when disciplined collaborative work produces one usable external path. Useful data or a safe partial calibration may resolve without the asset. Knowingly unsafe recovery, contaminated-network acceptance, or forced dramatic success after the risks are known may fail after informed action.

### Two Signatures

One required report establishes that the legal office is not deliberately malicious, existing systems cannot represent two equally continuous people, the agreement risks precedent that one Cross may consent for both, Helix Yard has a real repair need, and the decision belongs to Imani.

The player controls duty time, support, command pressure, emergency-use boundaries, formal recognition, privacy, and advocacy posture. The player does not choose Imani's signature, identity, or consent. The actual result may earn **Cross Isolation Protocol** only through Imani's independent consent to a narrow documented procedure with explicit limits and rights preserved. Her refusal or a rights-preserving delay may resolve responsibly without the asset. Coercion, substituted consent, or paternalistic override after the account is known may fail after informed action.

### Distributed readiness

One required campaign report aggregates the background development that occurs regardless of selected assignments:

- low-level false alerts and the narrowing activation window;
- an approved distributed command plan;
- defined independent responsibilities for senior officers without projecting each as its own tracker;
- the actual Tolland/Mercer support preserved from prior politics;
- Kessler's effort to hold the Compact coalition together;
- Prel's warning about contradictory Cardassian relief routes.

The report does not reveal the exact thirty-second activation trigger or Chapter 8's full alert list. It gates interval conclusion and the Chapter 8 transition so quiet assignments cannot skip campaign-critical preparation.

## Choices, Results, and Assets

Record only:

- one direct/delegated/declined engagement choice per assignment;
- one actual world-owned result per assignment: `assetEarned`, `resolvedWithoutAsset`, or `failedAfterInformedAction`;
- one player-owned interval conclusion: normal two, delegated broad coverage, direct overextension, or informed early departure.

Assignment choice and assignment result remain distinct. Delegation is not success. A direct approach is not automatically overextension until all three results exist. Decline does not become terminal until the player concludes the interval. The three durable assets are represented by the corresponding assignment result/dimension and carried in the transition packet; do not add duplicate ship-condition, quest, thread, command-log, or inventory rows merely because the asset is mentioned.

Significant Whitaker rapport, Imani trust, legal history, mentorship, and crew culmination remain Story Settlement/People material. The mission definition may preserve the assignment result without turning every relationship consequence into a mission dimension.

No Command Bearing award or spend is added in this slice. Source Inspiration/Resolve examples remain intent until the neutral reserve has a complete authored award contract.

## Projection and Closure

Expose four concise dimensions only:

- interval load and conclusion;
- The Name on the Hull result;
- A Signal Toward Home result;
- Two Signatures result.

Use no clock. The source supplies no player-known duration or fair deadline, and Chapter 7's task-group clock is already resolved or expired before this interval.

Close only from the required conclusion objective after the distributed-readiness report is known. Terminal dispositions should preserve:

- early departure with unfinished opportunities;
- serious informed assignment failure;
- direct-command overextension;
- broad regional capability when at least two assignments earn assets;
- a responsible interval fallback.

Serious informed failure outranks overextension and positive capability labels. Early departure outranks all assignment labels. One or more earned assets remain valid even when the interval closes with another cost.

## Chapter 8 Transition

Every terminal route targets the exact existing package quest `chapter-8-the-last-directive`. The transition may open with multiple regional alerts activating together and Whitaker assigning the player operational command, because those are now-present Chapter 8 events after interval closure.

Carry forward only assets actually earned, assignments actually resolved or declined, unfinished work, overextension, distributed-command readiness, and support preserved from prior politics. Do not invent a completed assignment, direct Voyager contact, universal Cross rights precedent, exact Nightfall node location, the three-node quorum, assigned finale officers, or an expected resolution strategy.

- stale revision, wrong swipe, and hallucinated-policy rejection;
- one quiet no-clue scene producing zero mission or legacy-tracker evidence;
- required report custody, multi-claim batching, source invalidation, restoration epoch, journey activation, reload, idempotency, and Chapter 8 pending state.
