## Fair Choice, Autonomous Consequence, and Partial Success

Process posture, Solenn disposition, and interface custody are exclusively user-sourced when voluntary. Assistant prose cannot prosecute, immunize, protect, seize, destroy, disclose, or withdraw for the player. Conversely, player prose cannot self-certify that a process formed, a witness entered custody, or the interface was secured. Actual results require accepted assistant/runtime/adjudicator evidence.

One player message may record both informed final choices because Solenn and interface policies are independent after all three discoverable facts are known. Choices alone do not complete accountability. Both actual results must be established.

The system distinguishes responsibility from bad luck:

- process collapse, witness flight, or interface loss before a final informed choice is `completedWithCost`, not player failure;

- responsible withdrawal can hand process, person, and interface authority to a named successor without inventing player choices;
- one assistant scene may settle the three related collapse or handoff results as one source contribution;
- deliberate destruction without preserving a record is covered as an informed accountability failure;
- Starfleet custody, local protection, immunity, restorative process, shared oversight, local sealing, and destruction with a record remain contextual outcomes, not universal moral answers.
